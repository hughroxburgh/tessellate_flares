Silver Sources

A silver source is a place on the sky with confident flares (pFlare ≥ 0.9) that cannot be pinned to a single Gaia star: usually two or more Gaia stars sit inside the flares' localisation regions and TESS's 21″ pixels can't tell them apart, or flares sit near a single Gaia star without being confidently on it. A star that had a confident flare is also counted here, not as a gold flare star, when most of the flares at its position can't be pinned to it. Silver sources are catalogued like the flare stars — flares, light curves and measurements — except that the host is given as a list of candidate Gaia stars with their probabilities.
